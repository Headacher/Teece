Teece

Adjacent enemy Teece compare touching edge numbers; the smaller one dies, ties do nothing, and every stat change anywhere re-checks the whole board. One Teece per turn; equips, magic and battlegrounds are unlimited. Win by wiping out every enemy Teece, ending a turn with four more Teece fielded (Tokens never count), or having the bigger field when a player must draw from an empty deck. Any Teece whose attributes drop below zero dies on the spot.

Classic

Both sides play the identical fixed twenty-card deck.

Teece 100

Draft your own twenty-card deck: twenty picks, three cards offered per pick, from every card in the game — the hundred draft designs plus all seventy-two deck cards. Ranged, Flying, Teleport, Defence, clocks, Mines, activated abilities (select your Teece on the board to use them), mind control and more.

Decks

Pick a prebuilt deck, choose which deck the computer brings, and set how hard it hits: every enemy Teece can carry anywhere from −3 to +3 on all four sides. Each deck has its own machinery: Berserker is twelve overpowered cards racing the clock, Rock soaks hits with Defence, Ogre shoves everything around the board and the damage it deals sticks.

Three players

A four by four board and three seats. Play a free-for-all where every other seat is an enemy, or take a side in two versus one. Allies never fight each other, they win and lose together, and a seat is skipped once its whole side is wiped out. Four Teece ahead of the next best side still wins it.

Roguelike

Draft five cards and beat the computer. Clear a level and you draft five more, but the enemy's Teece grow: +1 on every side at level two, +2 at level three, +3 at level four. Running out of cards never ends a level; only wiping out the enemy's Teece, or ending a turn four Teece ahead, does.
